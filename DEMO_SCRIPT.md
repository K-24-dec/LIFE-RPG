# ⚔️ LIFE RPG — Hackathon Live Demo Script (2:30 mins)

**Goal:** Present the problem, the solution, live working functionality, and technical architecture to hackathon judges within 2.5 minutes.

---

## 📊 Pre-Recording Quick Reference

- **Live Render URL**: `https://life-rpg-jlxb.onrender.com`
- **Local Dev Server**: `http://localhost:3000`
- **GitHub Repository**: `https://github.com/K-24-dec/LIFE-RPG`
- **Tech Stack**: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, HTML5 Canvas 60fps Particle Engine, Node/Express Authoritative Game Engine API, Supabase DB.
- **Demo Hero Accounts**:
  - **Account A (Returning Hero)**: Click `ACCOUNT A: RETURNING HERO (LVL 5)` on login page.
  - **Account B (Fresh Hero)**: Click `ACCOUNT B: FRESH NEW HERO (LVL 1)` on login page.

---

## 🎬 Minute-by-Minute Live Demo Script

### 0:00 – 0:20 | THE HOOK (The Problem)
* **Screen View**: Landing Page (`/`) showing the glowing **LIFE RPG** title and floating cosmic dust particle background.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"80% of productivity and habit apps are abandoned within 30 days. Why? Because they treat your goals like a cold, corporate to-do list — full of guilt, dry checkboxes, and zero motivation. We built **Life RPG** to solve this by turning your real-world daily habits into an immersive video game interface that you actually want to log into every single day."*

---

### 0:20 – 0:40 | THE SOLUTION (Product in One Line)
* **Screen View**: Click `[ ⚔ START YOUR JOURNEY ]` or `[ ACCOUNT A: RETURNING HERO ]` to transition smoothly into the **Adventure Hub (`/dashboard`)**.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Life RPG turns real-life tasks into active RPG quests. When you complete them, you earn XP and Gold, level up your character, build daily activity streaks, and spend your hard-earned gold in a Guild Market — all backed by proof of what you actually accomplished."*

---

### 0:40 – 2:00 | LIVE WALKTHROUGH (The Core 80 Seconds)

#### 1. Create a Quest (0:40 – 0:55)
* **Action**: Click `[ + CREATE QUEST ]` in the top right.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Let's say I want to study advanced code patterns. I hit Create Quest, set the title 'Master Async JavaScript', choose 'Coding', set a difficulty like Hard, and assign a 45-minute timer. Notice how it automatically calculates the reward: +75 XP, +35 Gold, and +5 Intelligence."*

#### 2. Start & Complete Quest with Proof (0:55 – 1:20)
* **Action**: Click `[ ⚔ START QUEST ]` on the card. Point out the live countdown timer HUD, then click `[ ✨ COMPLETE QUEST ]`.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Once I start the quest, a real-time countdown HUD begins. When I finish my study session, I click Complete Quest. To ensure zero cheating, I can attach a quick reflection note and upload proof — like a screenshot of my code — to verify the achievement."*

#### 3. Reward Feedback & Level-Up (1:20 – 1:45)
* **Action**: Submit completion. Watch the XP bar fill up, Gold count increase, streak flame glow, and trigger the **Level Up!** modal celebration.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"The moment I confirm, the authoritative backend processes the completion. I get instant XP and Gold, my Intelligence attribute rises, and my daily streak hits 7 days! And because my XP hit the threshold... BOOM — LEVEL UP! I'm now a Level 6 Hero with unlocked stat bonuses."*

#### 4. Guild Market & Arsenal Inventory (1:45 – 2:00)
* **Action**: Navigate to `🛒 Guild Market` (`/market`), buy an item (e.g. `Solar Gold Frame` or `Code Sorcerer` title), then switch to `🎒 Arsenal` (`/inventory`) and click `[ EQUIP ]`.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Now I can spend my earned gold in the Guild Market. I'll buy this Legendary Solar Gold Frame for 250 Gold, head to my Arsenal, and equip it to customize my character's command profile!"*

---

### 2:00 – 2:30 | WHY IT'S DIFFERENT & TECH STACK
* **Screen View**: Navigate to `🧙 Hero Profile` (`/character`) or `📜 Chronicle` (`/chronicle`) to highlight lifetime stats and verified proof logs.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"What makes Life RPG more than a gimmick is our architecture. Built on React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS with a 60fps HTML5 particle canvas, it's backed by a Node/Express authoritative game server and Supabase persistence. We validate timer minimums and proof uploads so you can't cheat your own streaks, providing genuine accountability."*

---

### 2:30 – 2:45 | THE CLOSE
* **Screen View**: Return to the **Adventure Hub (`/dashboard`)** for a clean, impressive final frame showing the glowing HUD.
* **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Life RPG turns daily discipline into visible, rewarding progress — making real-life self-improvement feel like playing your favorite video game. Thank you, and I'd love to take your questions!"*

---

## 🎥 Recording Pro-Tips

1. **Browser Zoom**: Set browser zoom to 100% or 110% so text and stats remain crisp on camera.
2. **Audio Tone**: Speak clearly and enthusiastically during the Level-Up transition.
3. **Cursor Flow**: Click deliberately on buttons (`START QUEST` $\to$ `COMPLETE QUEST` $\to$ `GUILD MARKET`).
